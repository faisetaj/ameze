About the studio

Beauty and wellness, optimized.

Welcome to Ameze Skin Elements, your premier destination for all your beauty needs in Houston, Texas. Before you visit

Appointments & the good-faith exam

Please arrive on time to ensure the full duration of your appointment — arrivals more than 15 minutes late may require rescheduling. A credit card remains on file through our encrypted system to secure your appointment, and all sales are final. Every treatment plan starts with a $39 good-faith exam that ensures treatments match your needs perfectly; the fee is credited toward treatment. The Richmond location operates exclusively on Fridays, and not all services are available there — contact us directly to book.

Cancellations

Appointments are reserved exclusively for you. We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a Thursday appointment, and 48 hours' notice for Friday and Saturday appointments — at the Richmond location as well. Cancellations without adequate notice incur a $50 fee, or $75 for same-day changes. Clients with valid packages may have one treatment deducted from the $50 cancellation fee.
