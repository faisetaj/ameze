The protocol

Great results are prepared, not improvised. The essentials below — your full instructions arrive with your booking.

Before your appointment
  1. Book at least 4 weeks ahead of any big event — results settle, bruising fades.
  2. Skip blood thinners & NSAIDs (aspirin, ibuprofen, fish oil, vitamin E) for 2 weeks if you can.
  3. No alcohol for 24 hours and no smoking for 48 hours before treatment.
  4. Pause Retin-A two days before and after. No dental work within 4 weeks.
  5. Eat a good breakfast and arrive with a clean, makeup-free face.
After your treatment
  1. No workouts, yoga or massage for 24 hours — keep the product where we placed it.
  2. No alcohol for 24 hours, no smoking for 48; both slow healing and invite bruising.
  3. Prone to bruising? Arnica cream at the injection site helps.
  4. Cleanse gently — no harsh scrubs or aggressive rubbing.
  5. We see you again in 2 weeks. The follow-up is part of the treatment, and it's free.
